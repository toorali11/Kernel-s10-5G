# Galaxy S10 and N10 Series Custom Kernels

## KSU Next 3.1.0 + SUSFS v2.0.0
Custom kernel for Samsung Galaxy devices with integrated KSU Next (v3.1.0) and SUSFS (v2.0.0).

## ✨ Features
* KSU Next v3.1.0 integration
* SUSFS v2.0.0 support
* Manual hook implementations
* Improved compatibility and stability

## 📱 Supported Devices
### Galaxy S10 Series
* beyond0lte (Galaxy S10e)
* beyond1lte (Galaxy S10)
* beyond2lte (Galaxy S10+)
* beyondx (Galaxy S10 5G)
* beyondxks (Galaxy S10 5G Korean)

### Galaxy Note10 Series
* d1 (Galaxy Note10)
* d1xks (Galaxy Note10 5G)
* d2s (Galaxy Note10+)
* d2x (Galaxy Note10+ 5G)
* d2xks (Galaxy Note10+ 5G Korean)

## 📦 Installation
* Download the compiled kernel ZIP
* Boot into TWRP recovery
* Flash the ZIP
* Reboot
  
## ⚠️ Notes
* Ensure your ROM and recovery are compatible
* Always take a full backup before flashing
 Flash at your own risk
